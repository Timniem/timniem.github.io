I am a PhD student in the System Genetics group at the University Medical Center Groningen (UMCG), supervised by Dr. K. Joeri van der Velde and Prof. Dr. Morris A. Swertz. My work focuses on developing and applying machine-learning methods to improve genome diagnostics, with a particular interest in rare disease variant interpretation.

My research focuses on the application of explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) for the interpretation of the effect of genetic variants. I am particularly interested in non-coding variantion, transcriptomics, and protein effect modeling, and integrating multi-omics data to better understand the molecular basis of rare diseases.
